# Rating guidelines (اردو کیپشن کی درجہ بندی)

You see one image, its English caption, and 2–3 Urdu versions (order is random; you never know which system wrote which). Rate **each version independently**.

**Adequacy (0–100)** — Does the Urdu say exactly what the English says, about *this image*?
- 100: same meaning, nothing added, nothing missing.
- 70: minor slip (a small detail off, e.g. "red" → "pink").
- 40: a main object/action is wrong or missing.
- 10: unrelated or mostly wrong.

**Fluency (0–100)** — Is it natural, grammatical Urdu a native speaker would write?
- 100: perfectly natural. 70: understandable but awkward word order or one agreement slip. 40: hard to read. 10: word salad or not Urdu.
- Judge fluency even if the meaning is wrong.

**Error tags** (tick all that apply): mistranslation · omission · addition (hallucinated content) · gender/number agreement · spelling/non-word (e.g. "ایلیف", "ابزار") · unnatural word order · untranslated English left in.

Rules: ~30 seconds per version; do not look up anything; do not "fix" in your head — rate what is written; accept standard Urdu loanwords (ٹرین، بس، پیزا). If the image does not load, rate against the English and tick nothing extra.
